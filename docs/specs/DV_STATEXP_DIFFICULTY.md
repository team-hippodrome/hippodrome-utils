# What it is
Statexp is the gen 1/2 equivalent to EVs and DVs are the gen 1/2 equivalent of IVs. The stadium games do have a variety of statexp and DVs for both rental pokemon and opponent pokemon.

This feature lets players set the minimum and maximum statexp and DVs rental and opponent pokemon can have. The sliders for rental and opponent pokemon should be listed and configurable separately.

This setting goes in the launcher and the Esc menu.

By default, the values should be set to "vanilla" to use vanilla settings.
