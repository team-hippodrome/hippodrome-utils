# What it is
Stadium 1's various versions had content cut or modified. This enhancement offers to restore that content if the user provides the required ROM.

## Nintendo Cup
> ROM required: Japanese

In the international release of Stadium 1, the Nintendo Cups were replaced with Poke Cup because the Nintendo Cup tournaments only took place in Japan. Stadion offers to restore this content by providing a Japanese ROM alongside an English ROM.

Both Poke Cup and Nintendo Cup 97 remain accessible when a Western and Japanese ROM are included. 

The announcer retained for each cup depends on the language the user is playing in:
- If the user is playing in Japanese, only Poke Cup should retain the announcer from the provided Western ROM.
- If the player is playing in a Western language, only the Nintendo Cups should use the Japanese announcer voice lines.

Because the Nintendo Cup 97 and Poke Cup icons are the same, the Nintendo Cup 97 icon should include a small Japanese flag drawn at runtime in its top-right corner. Likewise, the Poke Cup icon should have an American flag in its top right corner and a European Union flag in its top left corner.

### Unlocking methods for when using restored Nintendo/Poke Cup

The Mewtwo battle is unlocked when ALL of the following are cleared:
- Nintendo Cup 97
- Nintendo Cup 98
- Petite Cup
- Pika Cup
- Prime Cup Master Ball 
- Gym Leader Castle
- AND Either:
  - Nintendo Cup 99 Master Ball
  - Poke Cup Master Ball
  - or ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
    - Poke Cup Ultra Ball
    - Nintendo Cup 99 Ultra Ball

The Doduo Tower is unlocked when ANY of the following are clears:
- Prime Cup Master Ball
- Nintendo Cup 99 Master Ball
- Poke Cup Master Ball
- or ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
  - Poke Cup Ultra Ball
  - Nintendo Cup 99 Ultra Ball

The Dodrio Tower is unlocked when ALL of the following are clears:
- Prime Cup
- ANY of the following:
  - Nintendo Cup 99 Master Ball
  - Poke Cup Master Ball
  - or ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
    - Poke Cup Ultra Ball
    - Nintendo Cup 99 Ultra Ball


## Gallery
> ROM required: English (any version)
Gallery mode was exclusive to the US release of Stadium 1, although the full data exists in the European English release. It allows players to take pictures of their Pokemon. This enhancement allows non-English ROMs to access gallery mode if the user provides an English ROM alongside their non-English ROM.

## Nidoqueen Animation
> Version Required: Japanese
Nidoqueen had a different animation where she gropes her breasts in the Japanese version. Either animation can be toggled on if a Japanese ROM is provided; otherwise, only the animation from the provided ROM is offered.

## Jynx Color
> Version Required: US 1.0 or Japanese for original color, other version for modern color.
Jynx had its color changed from black to purple in later releases of Stadium 1. Either color can be toggled on if a Japanese ROM or US 1.0 is provided; otherwise, only the color from the provided ROM is offered.
